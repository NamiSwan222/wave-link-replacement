WaveFix: mic effects that stay out of your Discord stream

WaveFix is the control panel. Your effects run inside Windows' own audio engine through
Equalizer APO, which is why your mic can't leak into Discord streams the way it does with
Wave Link.

Before your effects work, you need (one time):

  1. Equalizer APO (free): https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/
     In its Device Selector, tick only your real microphone on the "Capture devices" tab.

  2. The noise removal plugin (free, RNNoise):
     https://github.com/werman/noise-suppression-for-voice/releases
     Put rnnoise_mono.dll (from the "vst" folder) into:
     C:\Program Files\EqualizerAPO\VSTPlugins\rnnoise\

  3. So Discord's default mode gets the effects, run tools\run-as-system.ps1 from the
     project as administrator, then restart Windows Audio (or reboot).

Full guide: https://github.com/NamiSwan222/wave-link-replacement

WaveFix tells you on its main screen if any of these steps is missing.
WaveFix updates itself from GitHub; you can turn that off in the app.
