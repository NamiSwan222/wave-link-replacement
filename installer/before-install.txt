WaveFix: mic effects that stay out of your Discord stream

WaveFix is the control panel. Your effects run inside Windows' own audio engine through
WaveFix's audio engine, which is why your mic can't leak into Discord streams the way it does
with Wave Link.

Setup is automatic. The first time you open WaveFix it shows a "Set it up for me"
button that, after one Windows permission prompt:

  1. Installs WaveFix's audio engine
  2. Adds the RNNoise noise removal plugin (free, from GitHub)
  3. Connects the engine to the microphone you pick (its existing effects keep working)
  4. Restarts Windows audio (your sound drops for a few seconds)

Used WaveFix with Equalizer APO before? The same button switches you over and removes it.

The download is checked against a known fingerprint before it's used, and steps that
are already done are skipped.

Full guide: https://github.com/NamiSwan222/wave-link-replacement
WaveFix updates itself from GitHub; you can turn that off in the app.
